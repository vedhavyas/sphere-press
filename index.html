About Sphere Press

Sphere Press is an independent publishing imprint. We make original large-print coloring books for adults and seniors, big-and-simple coloring books for toddlers, and the occasional guided journal. Every book is designed, drawn and reviewed by hand before it goes to print.

Our illustrations are created with AI assistance and reviewed page by page. We keep the lines bold, the shapes simple and the pages single-sided, so the books are comfortable to use at any age.

Prices and availability may change. Prices shown are in US dollars.
